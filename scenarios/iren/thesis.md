## What defines each case [DOC]

- **Bear:** Deliveries slip and the build is 400 MW in each of 2027 and 2028, with a 30% haircut on signed-but-unbuilt capacity. The build is partly funded by selling about $2.5B of equity at roughly $35 in 2027 and $1.0B at roughly $33 in 2028.
- **Base:** The 2027 target of about 500 MW is met (455 MW new plus 45 MW already signed), followed by 700 MW in 2028. There is a 20% haircut, and about $1.5B of equity is sold at roughly $70 in 2027 with none in 2028.
- **Bull:** 2028 is largely pre-sold by end-2027, with about 300 MW signed but unbuilt (5.9B credited). The haircut is 10% and almost no equity is sold.

## How the numbers connect

Enterprise value is credited ARR times the multiple; equity value is enterprise value less net debt (bear 27.6, base 32.1, bull 41.8 for 2027); value per share is equity value over the solved share count. The table figures were checked against these relations on 2026-10-26 and agree to rounding.
