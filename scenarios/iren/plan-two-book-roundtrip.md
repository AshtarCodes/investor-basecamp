## Books

- **Return book (R).** Outright Jan 2029 calls that pass the 200-point test, and no sleeve that fails it. Aim: a base-case return worth taking option risk over holding the stock.
- **Barbell book (B).** The bear cushion (25/75 spreads, held or sold; deep-ITM calls) plus a small far-OTM call. It is exempt from the 200-point test, because it is there for the bear and the bull, not the base.
- **Stock book (S).** Shares, which are also the first source of the 2028 debt tranche.

Capital splits to sweep on $10,000: R/B/S of 50/25/25, 40/30/30, 60/20/20, 30/30/40, and each book alone. Contracts are whole; shares take the remainder, as in the earlier $10,000 sizing.

A preview from the earlier run, steady-path scale-out base returns against the stock's +468% (Jan 2029, sold in 2028): the $25 call is +244 points, the $40 call +376, the $90 call +458, the 40/90 spread +205, and the $15 call, the 25/75, 25/90 and 40/75 spreads fall short. On the "rally already arrived" path (stock +547%) the 40/90 spread falls to +159 and fails; the three outright calls still pass. For the Jan 2028 programme (stock +195% steady, +306% front) only the 40/100 and 40/140 spreads pass on the steady path; the $25, $40 and $75 calls pass only on the front path.

## Paths and policies

Paths, with the spot on the sale or peak date and at year-end (Jun and Nov peaks, as before):

| Path | Peak | Year-end | Source of the numbers |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Straight base | base value | base value | earlier runs |
| Straight bull | bull value | bull value | earlier runs |
| Straight bear | n/a | bear value | earlier runs |
| Round trip, base | base value at the peak date | bear value | new |
| Round trip, bull | bull value at the peak date | bear value | new |

The bear case is a mix of a straight fall and a round trip. Default 20% straight, 80% round trip, per the owner's view; this is a parameter.

Policies, applied to each construction on each path:

1. **Hold:** the earlier early-sale rule (half sold in June, half in November).
2. **Half-sale:** at the peak date sell half the calls and hold the other half to the year-end (sold if still alive, or run to expiry for spreads, both modelled).
3. **Half-sale plus puts:** as 2, and buy puts with the proceeds on the remaining calls' and shares' delta. Hedge ratios 50% and 100%.
4. **Puts only:** hold everything and buy the same puts at the peak, paid for by selling shares.

Hedge trigger: the stock at $85 and, separately, at the 2027 base case of $103, on the June and November 2027 dates. There is no 2028 hedge: the owner does not have enough information to set a 2028 level. Puts bought in 2027 on Jan 2029 positions are valued at the 2028 sale dates; puts on Jan 2028 positions end at the 2027 year-end.

Put model: listed Jan 2028 and Jan 2029 puts, strikes nearest the hedge level (at the money and about 15% below). A put's price at the peak date is its model value at that date and spot; its payoff is the model value at year-end and the bear spot. Three IV assumptions for the hedge, since the cost of hedging into a rally is the least certain input: Rule 2 (puts cheap after a rally), flat, and flat plus 10 vol points. Pay model value plus half of today's quoted spread, in percent.

## Debt tranche

The money is needed on 2027-11-30, which is already a sale date in the earlier runs. The amount is a share of the return the $10,000 has generated by then on that path: withdraw 10%, 25% or 50% of (portfolio value on 2027-11-30 minus $10,000), and nothing on a path with no gain. So the dollars vary by path and by construction; the report shows them. Funding order: calls first, riskiest first (far-OTM calls, then OTM, then ATM and deep-ITM calls, then spreads), and shares last; calls are sold only until the amount is met, so the rest of the position stays on. Selling the riskiest calls first de-risks the book as a side effect. The owner does not need to sell everything. Calls sold for the debt are not available for the half-sale or the hedge, so the tranche shrinks the position the policies act on, and the half-sale rule applies to what is left. This also means Jan 2029 options must be valued on 2027-11-30, with 14 months left, which the earlier runs did not do; it is new work. Outputs per path: dollars raised, dollars still short, and what remains invested afterwards. Tax on a sale is not modelled (options bought in October 2026 and sold before October 2027 would be short-term gains); check that with whoever does the owner's tax.

## Metrics

- Base-case excess return over the stock, in percentage points (the 200-point test), per sleeve and for each book.
- Result on every path, in dollars and percent of $10,000, for each construction and policy.
- Round-trip give-back: the result at year-end against the result at the peak date.
- The thesis-weighted result with the bear split into straight and round trip.
- Debt tranche: dollars raised and shortfall.
- Hedge cost at the peak date, in percent of the position it covers, under each IV assumption.
